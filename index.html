RG Flow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

In short: RG Flow does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or usage data. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

What the app accesses, and why

RG Flow is an offline music player. To build your library of albums, artists and songs, it reads your device's local media library (via Android's READ_MEDIA_AUDIO permission) and, where available, the embedded cover art stored inside your own audio files. This information is used only to display and play your music on your device — it is never copied anywhere else, and the app has no way of doing so (see below).

No internet access

RG Flow does not request Android's internet permission and cannot make network requests of any kind. There is no server, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no advertising SDK built into the app. Nothing about your music library, your listening activity, or your device ever leaves your device.

No accounts, no third parties

RG Flow does not require you to create an account or sign in, and it does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect or transmit any data in the first place.

Children's privacy

Since RG Flow collects no data of any kind from any user, this applies equally to children as it does to adults.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
